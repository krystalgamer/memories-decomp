==== USER CONTEXT ==== 

With the emulator in interpreter mode I injected the script at the main menu and started a duel against Villager2.

I played a Sogen which played the animation of the duel board turning into "MEADOW". For the transition animation it simulates a light effect where the board glows white and few white blobs move up.

For the first 3D fight my "Dark Fire Dragon" (id 168) attacks "Megirus Light". The animation was similar to what I described in "model_slot_slate.txt" but on Meadow environment.
My dragon tossed a fire ball at Megirus and it just dropped and faded out. Then my dragon growled and the screen faded and the enemy card "blew up". This animation is exclusive for 3D battles, when we come back to the duel board the destroyed card plays a explosion animation.

Attacking directly has no 3D animation so nothing happened.

For the next attack it was my "The Wicked Worm Beast" (id 23) against a "Greenkappa" (id 586), which was in defense. Don't really remember how it attacked but I know Greenkappa survived the attack and the defense animation was the bipyramid I had described in "model_slot_slate.txt". After getting attacked it just faded out and stayed on board.


The trace was printed while I was trying to finish the duel, when my Dragon Zombie was attacking the enemy card (no 3D battle).
