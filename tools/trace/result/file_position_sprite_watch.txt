==== USER CONTEXT ==== 

With the emulator in Interpreter mode I booted the game and on the main-menu after loading the save I injected the script and then did the "Soft Reset". A bunch of stuff started being printed but it was when it reached "vs=565" that the screen became black and the Konami logo showed (regular boot).

I let the boot sequence play out and loaded a save, went into Library, Campaign (into the overworld), back to the main-menu, to the 2P Duel screen, loaded the save again then started a duel against "Duel Master K". This is where the trace was printed.

I never saw an icon on the bottom-ride of the screen during the loads.
