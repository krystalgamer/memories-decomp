==== USER CONTEXT ==== 

With the emulator in interpret mode I injected the script at the main menu and then started a fight against Duel Master K. Quick note, the trace only printed at the end so I have no way of correlating each line with what was on screen. Also didn't manage to do a single fusion during the game.

For the first 3D battle my "Mystical Sheep #1" (id 642) attacked "Twin-headed Thunder Dragon" (id 613), which was in defense position. Since no field was set the battle happened at the default arena. The camera starts spinning and then stops at a spot where a couple circles with different colors rotate on the floor, merge into one and fire starts appearing around the circumference. There my monster fades in and then it pans to the enemy monster. The sheep attacked by turning its back and sending a bunch of circles in different colors and the enemy monster defended by making a bipyramid appear.
Since my monster was too weak they just faded out and the battle ended with no monster destroyed.

The other battle was between my "Queen Bird" (id 468) and "Twin-headed Thunder Dragon" but this time in attack position. Since my opponent had played the mountain card before we loaded in the mountain field and the animation with the circles was the same. There Queen Bird headbutted twin-headed and then twin-headed headbutted it back. Since my monster was weaker it played a falling animation and that's when the trace was printed.
