## `Main_Init` at 0x80012B50

`gcc_2_8_1_g8_split`, 97 instructions against a target of 97, opcode distance
0, positional opcode diff 4.

The boot path. It resets the kernel callback state inside a critical section,
clears the display, zeroes a block of resident state, brings up the subsystem
initialisers, installs the vertical-blank callback, seeds the RNG, arms the
frontend restart point with `setjmp`, and finally enters `Main_Loop`.

Three facts carried this from a reordered store block to exact opcode distance.

**The resident state words are `volatile`.** Written as plain externs, GCC
reorders the whole block: it hoists the two non-zero stores above the zero
stores and groups the zeroes together, which costs fifteen positions. Marking
them `volatile` pins every store to source order and reproduces the target's
interleaving exactly. A sibling already declares `D_8009B09C` this way, and the
same applies to its neighbours.

**The reload of `D_8009B09C` is a separate read into a local.** The target
stores zero and then immediately loads the same word back, with the store to
`D_8009B0C4` deferred to the end of the block. That is not a store that failed
to forward: it is a read taken at that point into a temporary and consumed
later. Adding the temporary moves the load from the end of the block to
directly after the store and removes four more positions.

**Three globals need three different addressing forms.** The state words are
plain scalar externs and reach `%gp_rel` under `-G8`. `D_8009B230` and
`D_8009B269` carry `section(".data")`, which puts them outside small data and
produces the `$at` macro form the target uses for both byte stores.
`D_8009B4A8`, `D_800E9EC0` and `D_800E9DC0` are arrays whose addresses are
taken, so they split into compiler registers.

`libgte.h` must be included before `libgpu.h`, matching the sibling display
sources; on its own `libgpu.h` does not parse.

## What is left

Four positions, all in the same place: the target computes `&D_8009B4A8` and
materialises the constant 1 before the store block, while this build emits the
address next to its use and the constant just before its first store. Every
instruction from the `D_8009B0D1` store onward is positionally identical,
including both `$at`-form byte stores and the whole call tail.

Tried without hoisting them: assigning the address to a local before the block,
routing the constant through a local as well, both together, dropping
`volatile` from the destination of the address store so the computation is free
to float, and every GCC 2.8.1 profile. The scheduling of those two setup
instructions did not move.
