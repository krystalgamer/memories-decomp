### What the shape needed

- **The dispatch is a `switch` on `status & 0xF0`.** GCC builds the retail
  decision tree — compare against `0xB0`, then `slti 177` to split the
  remaining four cases — only from a switch.
- **The `0x7F8`-is-zero exits are `else` arms, not early returns.** Written as
  `if (... == 0) { track[38] = data2; return; }` GCC makes the return block the
  fall-through and pushes the loop out of line; retail has the opposite. An
  `else` after the loop reproduces the retail layout and is worth two
  positions.
- **The state pointer is read once per iteration and used for both the record
  address and the loop bound.** Reading `D_8009B458` separately for
  `p = (u8 *)D_8009B458 + off` and for `D_8009B458->field_07FA` gives two loads
  per iteration, because the stores through `p` may alias the pointer itself
  and block the CSE. One local read at the top of the body serves both and
  takes the build from eleven instructions long to four.

### What is left

Four instructions, all load-delay `nop`s around the per-iteration state read.
Retail places that read between the byte copies and the word copies, where the
delay slots fill naturally; every source placement tried puts it at the top of
the body and needs a `nop` before the record address and another before the
loop bound compare.

The entry copies also rotate: retail binds the track record to `$t5`, the
channel to `$t4` and the first data byte to `$t3`, this build to `$t4`, `$t3`
and `$t5`. Pinning the three closes that but does not change the count, so it
is recorded here rather than in the stored source.
