## `func_80060E70` at 0x80060E70

`gcc_2_8_1_cc_g0_as_g8_split`, 102 instructions against a target of 102, opcode
distance 6, positional opcode diff 9.

Builds the seven text boxes for one duel hand row. The caller passes the entry
list, the row index, and a flag. The row record is `D_801A8000[idx]`, a 24-byte
struct holding a display object pointer, an x/y pair, the flag, and a seven-slot
`u16` array of card ids. Each iteration copies the entry id into that array,
picks a box style, creates the box, positions it at a 0x16 pitch, and hands it
to `func_80039A14`.

The style is the interesting part. It starts as the id itself, so an empty slot
yields style zero and the whole lookup block is skipped. For a live card the
style is `0x18`, or `0x1A` when the row flag is set, plus one more when the card
type in the top five bits of `gDuel_adwCardStats[id - 1]` is at least 0x14. The
type is extracted with an arithmetic shift, which is why that table is `s32` and
not `u32`.

The `Box` struct, `TextBox_Create` signature and `func_80039A14` are taken
unchanged from the matched `src/game/card_list_text_boxes.c`, which builds the
same kind of row and already pins `field34`, `field3A` and `field54`.

`gDuel_wSelectedCardID` needs `section(".data")` here to take the `$at` macro
form; the sibling declares it plainly because in that translation unit it is
reached through `%gp_rel`.

## The next lever

The target sets up **two** cursors into the entry list before the loop, `$s3`
over the ids and `$s1 = $s3 + 2` over the flags, and keeps a plain counter for
the bound (`slti $v0, $s2, 0x7`). This build indexes `e[i]` from a single base,
so GCC strength-reduces the counter into a pointer comparison and never emits
the `addiu $s1, $s3, 0x2`.

Spelling the two cursors explicitly does reach opcode distance 3, down from 6,
which confirms the reading. That variant is recorded below as the alternative
base. It is not the one stored because advancing them as `u16 *` by two elements
costs an extra instruction (103 against 102) and moves the callee-saved register
saves, taking the positional diff from 9 to 16. The cursors want a spelling that
advances by one entry without adding an induction variable.

The loop bound is not fixed by `-fno-strength-reduce`: both profiles that carry
it come out longer, at 105 and 101 instructions, and worse on every measure.

## Alternative base at distance 3

Replace the loop body with two `u16 *` cursors initialised to `&e->id` and
`&e->flag`, each advanced by 2 elements per iteration, reading `idp[0]` and
`flagp[0]` in place of `e[i].id` and `e[i].flag`.
