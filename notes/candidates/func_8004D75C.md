## `func_8004D75C` at 0x8004D75C

`gcc_2_8_1_g0`, 110 of 110 instructions, opcode distance 2, 65 differing
positions.

The sound command-list interpreter for one channel. For each active slot it
walks a command stream out of the `0xDD8` list: a negative word selects the row
in bits 16..22 and, if that row's per-slot key is still 0xFFFF, claims it and
follows the low half as the next stream offset; a non-negative word accumulates
the byte at `+2` into that row's per-slot counter. Afterwards it takes the
maximum of each of nine rows' counters into the slot at the end of the row.

Struct shape, all recovered here: the channel stride is 0xE20, the slot pointer
array is at 0x1E0, the per-slot key rows are at 0x2C8 with a **116**-byte row
stride, the counter rows are at 0x750 with a **118**-byte stride whose last
`u16` (offset 116) holds that row's maximum, the stream list pointer is at
0xDD8 and the active-slot count at 0xE1B. The two arrays deliberately have
different row strides; reading them as one shape does not reproduce the
addressing.

Four things are settled:

- **The inner walker must be `while (1)`, not `for (;;)`.** This is the whole
  difference between 98 differing and 70. With `for (;;)` GCC does not hoist the
  0xFFFF sentinel out of the two loops and rematerialises `li 0xffff` inside the
  inner loop, where the target has `ori $t5, $zero, 0xFFFF` in the outer
  preheader. Same body, same count, and the two spellings are supposed to be
  identical.
- **The command word is unsigned.** `(word >> 16) & 0x7F` on an `s32` gives
  `sra`; the target has `srl`, so the shifted operand is `u32` and the sign test
  is a separate `(s32)word < 0`.
- **The sentinel test is `!= 0xFFFF` with the `cmd++` arm first.** The target
  branches `beq $v0, $t5` to the claiming arm, so the advance is the
  fall-through.
- `row = 1` must be assigned **before** `slot->field_001A = 1`, so the two share
  one materialised constant -- the target uses `$a1` for both.

**What is left** is two positions and they are the same disagreement seen from
both ends. The guard `if (ch->count != 0)` around the `for` is what makes GCC
hoist the sentinel, but it also emits a second `beqz` on the same loaded count
that the target does not have. Dropping the guard removes the duplicate test and
the hoist together and lands at 108. The target has one test *and* the hoist.
Against that, the target spends its extra instruction on the addressing: it
keeps a byte-offset induction variable at `0x74 + 2i` and forms the address as
`addu $v0, $a3, $t3` / `sh $a0, 0x2C8($v0)`, where this build folds the constant
into one displacement off a `ch + 2i` pointer. So the counts happen to agree at
110 with the two differences cancelling.

Crossed without improving on it: five spellings of the outer loop (`for` with
and without the guard, an explicit `do`/`while`, a zero-initialised guard), the
sentinel as a named local and as a `register` local (GCC constant-propagates it
back either way), flat `u16` arrays against the two-dimensional form against an
explicit byte-offset cast through a re-based `Channel *`, and both `g0` and `g8`.
