**What the shape needed.**

- *The position tables are multi-dimensional* (`s16 D_800907AC[2][2][4]`,
  `DuelFieldPosition D_80090800[2][20]`), so the side, page and index
  terms are separate products added to the base, not one folded index.

  stores can float past its stores, and the vertical `target_y = table.y`
  store followed by the `(s16)target_y` read folds into the register with
  an explicit `sll`/`sra` sign extension.
- *The glide shift is one function-scope variable* (16 or 8 in the row
  move, written `shift = 0x10; if (... != 3) shift = 8;`); a block-local
  single-set variable is launch-delayed past the call and lands in a

- *The `D_8009B162` timer store precedes the step store* in both blocks.
