## `func_80048A28` at 0x80048A28

`gcc_2_8_1_cc_g0_as_g8`, 118 instructions against a target of 121, opcode
distance 5, positional opcode diff 15.

The three-argument form of the sound-driver pan update. It is the same function
as `func_80048768` with a second value carried through: bit 0x8000 of the key
forwards to `func_80044E90` with the pan sign-extended, and the
`0xF000 == 0x4000` form is an indirect key resolved through two tables off
`g_SDValue`, abandoning the call on either `0xFFFF`. The main pass walks the
four voices and, for the voice whose key at +0x404 matches, scales the sample's
base level at +0x444 by the distance either side of centre.

This candidate was adapted from the stored `func_80048768` entry rather than
derived from scratch, which is what put it at distance 5 on the first compile.

## What differs from the two-argument form

**The second argument is a byte written into the voice record.** It lands at
+0x424 indexed by the voice number directly, not by the doubled index the level
tables use, and it is written as soon as the envelope test passes, before the
pan is examined at all.

**The early-exit arm also publishes it.** On the 0x8000 path the low byte of
that argument is stored as a halfword at +0x510 before the forward call.

## What is left

Three instructions and fifteen positions. Two of the three are a reload of
`g_SDValue` in the far-window arm: the target loads the global again there
where this build keeps the pointer it already holds. Forcing the reload through
a second local, with and without hoisting the window test into its own
variable, does not reproduce it and scores worse.

are expected to behave the same way here: the `0x100` term if-converts to a
branch instead of the target's `andi`, `sltu`, `sll` sequence, the `0xFFFF`
comparison constant takes the `addiu` rather than `ori` form, and the far
