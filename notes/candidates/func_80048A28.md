## What differs from the two-argument form

+0x424 indexed by the voice number directly, not by the doubled index the level
tables use, and it is written as soon as the envelope test passes, before the
pan is examined at all.

that argument is stored as a halfword at +0x510 before the forward call.

distance 5 to 3. The target re-reads `g_SDValue` inside the near arm as well as
the far one, rather than reusing the pointer it already holds from the key

two-argument form wants, leaves the build three instructions short here.
