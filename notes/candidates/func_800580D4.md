## Four things the shape needed

**The slot walk is the standard model-slot stride.** The prologue's shift
chain computes `index * 0xE20`, which is `MODEL_SLOT_SIZE`, so this reuses the
`(u8 *)D_800F2C40 + index * MODEL_SLOT_SIZE` convention from the five matched
siblings rather than inventing a struct.

**The later reads go through the TABLE BASE, not the slot.** `lw $v0,
0xD18($s2)` is `D_800F2C40[0]`, not the indexed slot. Folding those into the
slot pointer is wrong.

**Those three reads must not be CSE'd.** The target reloads the `+0xD18`
pointer before each use; GCC assumes the intervening store through `arg3`
cannot alias the table and keeps one copy. Reading through
`*(u8 * volatile *)` forces the reloads. The placement is specific --
qualifying the pointer works, while `volatile u8 *` on the object and
`volatile u16 *` on the halfword load are both completely inert.

**The `0x1000` fixed-point constant must be inlined at all four uses.** With a
named local GCC re-materialises it (`li a2,4096`) before the call; inlined, it
keeps one register live across the three subtractions and lets the final
`subu` write into `$a2` as the outgoing argument. Worth 5 positions.

Two stack blocks are struct assignments rather than loops or `strcpy`: the
0x20-byte copy from `co.coord` to `co.workm`, and the 8-byte `lwl/lwr` +
`swl/swr` block. `sp+0x10` is a `GsCOORDINATE2` (`flg` plus two `MATRIX` is
0x44, and its address is stored to `arg3+0x4C`); `arg3+0x4` is a `MATRIX`
whose `t[]` lands exactly on the three zero stores at `+0x18`/`+0x1C`/`+0x20`.

## Crossed without improving on the register swap

Binding `arg3` to a local to start its live range before the table base,
assigned both first and after the slot pointer. An explicit local for
`arg3 + 4` (worse: 125 instructions, distance 6, on both profiles). Making the
two `&work` uses textually distinct so CSE cannot share them. Moving the
`arg3` zero-store earlier to change pressure (worse). `scratch` at 0x20, 8 and
4 bytes and as an `SVECTOR`. Dropping the `pad5C` local. Merging the two
8-byte vectors into a 2-element array. All 29 profiles. All of the local-layout
axes were re-swept after the profile moved, since a base change reopens them.

The frame comes out 0xF8 against the target's 0xD8, and no local declaration
changes that, so the extra 0x20 is not a declared object.
