### Why the canonical ledger never got here

All six canonical attempts ended in a GCC 2.8.1 `cc1` signal-11 ICE and never
reached code generation. Each one rebuilt the model slot as a fresh oversized
struct or as an explicit `0xE20` byte stride. Reusing the tracked `ModelSlot`
from `model.h` and the 24-byte `Record` already declared in `func_800533D8.c`
compiles cleanly, so the ICE was a property of those recovered declarations
rather than of the function.

### The three shape facts

**`-fno-strength-reduce` is required.** At plain `-O2` GCC builds a *third*
induction variable at `r + 20` and reaches every record field with negative
displacements (`sh zero,-8(t1)`, `swl t7,-1(t1)`). Retail keeps one cursor and
positive displacements `0x0`..`0x17`. No source spelling defeats this: named
struct members, `*(s16 *)((u8 *)r + N)` casts, a body-local alias pointer, and
`while`/`for`/`goto`/byte-offset scans all reproduce the extra induction
variable. Disabling strength reduction reproduces the retail loop body exactly,
including `swl t6,19(t0)` / `swr t6,16(t0)`.

**The scan bound must be relative to the cursor.** Written as the literal
`D_800F2B50 + 10` GCC materialises a second address constant with `lui`/`addiu`
*inside* the loop. Written as `end = r + 10` it becomes the retail
`addiu v1,t0,240`, hoisted once before the scan.

**The fill reads its own copy of the running offset.** `base[off + i + 1]`
uses the scan's offset directly; retail copies it first (`move a1,t1`). A
separate `k = off;` immediately before the eight-byte loop reproduces that
copy.

A dead four-byte local additionally reproduces the eight-byte frame
(`addiu sp,sp,-8` / `addiu sp,sp,8`), but it arrives as `vars=8, regs=0/0`
rather than the retail `vars=0, regs=1/0`.

### What remains

Retail allocates fifteen hard registers — `v0`, `v1`, `a0`-`a3`, `t0`-`t7` and
`s0` — where this candidate allocates thirteen and stops at `t6`. `$s0` is
saved and restored but never read or written in between, so it is a pseudo that
survived allocation and then lost its only insn. Two extra conflicting pseudos
are needed to reach it, and the two visible consequences are the same shortage:
retail computes `a4 << 1` inside the body while this build hoists it out of the
scan, and retail keeps the fill counter in `a0` while this build reuses the
bound register `v1`.

`volatile` does not help either: the record array, the record's flag field,
the model slot pointer and the record cursor were each declared `volatile` in
turn, and none changes the register count. That rules out the store/reload
signature as the cause here — retail's `$s0` is saved and restored with no
access in between, so there is no memory operation to restore.

Explored without reaching it: every `gcc_2_8_1_*` profile; `do`/`while`,
`while`, `for(;;)`, `goto` and counted scans; pointer, byte-offset and
byte-base bounds; struct members against pointer casts; staging the `Word4`
stores through locals; `bits` as `s32` against `u8 *`; the fill constant and
the fifth argument held in their own locals; a `u32` fill counter; a
`do`/`while` fill; and `register` variables pinned to `$16`, which GCC drops
