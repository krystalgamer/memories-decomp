## `func_80042C08` at 0x80042C08

`gcc_2_8_1_g8`, 344 instructions against a target of 348, **opcode multiset
distance 4**. The first 87 instructions are exact, the frame is retail's
(0x58, nine saved registers), the seven spill slots are at retail's offsets,
and every register role matches. What is left is four instructions.

The gouraud "burst" builder. It lays eight `POLY_G4` into the PSX scratch pad
as four quadrant pairs on two rings, sweeps them through four angular steps of
`0x100`, and hands each quad to `func_8005B260` once per step. The quads start
degenerate -- every `y` at the object's centre -- and at the end of each step
the far edge (`x2`/`y2`, `x3`/`y3`) is promoted to the near edge (`x0`/`y0`,
`x1`/`y1`) with two word copies, so the eight quads trail the sweep.

Scratch pad layout, all three bases derived from one `lui` of `0x1F800000`:

| address | contents |
| --- | --- |
| `0x1F800000` | three `s32` working radii, reused by the `x` pass and the `y` pass |
| `0x1F800010` | the two `s16` scale factors off the object, widened to `s32` |
| `0x1F800020` | eight `POLY_G4`, `0x24` each |

Quad index bit 0 chooses the `x` sign, bit 1 the `y` sign and bit 2 the ring.
Quads 0..3 are the inner ring and take radii `r[0]`/`r[1]` with black outer
vertices; 4..7 are the outer ring, take `r[1]`/`r[2]` and are white throughout.
The three radii are `object->unk48`, `object->unk4A` and the constant `240`,
each scaled by `rcos`/`rsin` of the step and then by the matching factor, every
product rounded with the `bgez` / `addiu 0xFFF` / `sra 12` sequence GCC emits
for a signed `/ 4096`.

**The profile is `-O2`.** Every `-O2` variant is byte-identical here and first
diverges at instruction 87; `-O1` diverges at instruction 1 and
`no_sched1`/`no_sched2` at 1-4. This function touches no globals, so the `-G`
and `-msplit-addresses` axes are inert and all twelve `cc_g*_as_g*` profiles
give the same bytes. The matched neighbours `func_80042B98` and `func_80042BC0`
are `gcc_2_8_1_g8`, which agrees.

## Levers, in the order they paid

**Fold the centre coordinate into the load, and do not funnel every value
through one temporary.** Written `h = *(u16 *)&r[n]; q->x0 = h + cx;` GCC gives
the single variable `h` a single hard register, so it cannot issue two loads
back to back and every one of them takes a `nop`: nine unfilled load-delay
slots that retail does not have. Written `h = *(u16 *)&r[n] + cx; q->x0 = h;`
the load and the add land in one register, and with **two** temporaries
alternating across the six groups GCC can hold two sums live and pair the
loads, which is retail's `lhu $v0, 8($s4)` / `lhu $v1, 0($s4)` back to back.
Worth 15 positions and it is the single biggest lever here. The winning
assignment is groups 1-4 on one temporary and groups 5-6 on the other, the same
in all three store blocks; all 32 two-temporary patterns were tried.

**Subscript the per-quad emit loop; do not walk a cursor.** `p = poly; ... p++`
makes GCC bias the induction variable by 16 (the first member offset) so the
copies come out at 8/16/-8/0, and it keeps a *second* permanent copy of the
array base in a callee-saved register, which costs the register retail spends
on `&poly[5]`. Plain `poly[j]` subscripting gives retail's unbiased
`addiu $s0, $s0, 0x24` and frees the register. This is the #1546 lever and the
#1560 refinement of it applying at the same time.

**Name five quad pointers, and only five.** Retail materialises `&poly[1]`,
`&poly[4]`, `&poly[5]`, `&poly[6]`, `&poly[7]`, spills four of them to
`0x1C`-`0x28(sp)` and keeps `&poly[5]` in `$fp`, while quads 0, 2 and 3 are
rematerialised as `$s2 + imm`. Plain subscripting everywhere gives GCC bases it
can analyse, so it hoists all six `lhu` above all sixteen `sh` and spills
nothing (325 instructions, 23 short, zero reloads against retail's 17). Naming
all eight overshoots to 418. An exhaustive sweep of every subset of size 3-6
containing 1, 5 and 7 puts the floor at exactly retail's set.

**The third radius respells `sc[0]`** rather than reusing the hoisted copy the
first two share; hoisting all three is one instruction short and moves the
first divergence from 87 back to 80. `cx` and `cy` are `s32` locals -- as `s16`
they load with `lhu` where the target uses `lh`.

**The store order is not a choice.** Retail stores `x0` for quads 1,3,5,7, then
`x1` for the same four, then `x0` and `x1` for 0,2,4,6. Written that way GCC's
CSE produces retail's value grouping (two stores for the first radius, four for
the second, two for the third) with nothing further to arrange.

## What is left

Four instructions, all present in retail and absent here:

- one `addiu $a0, $s2, 0x6C`. Retail materialises `&poly[3]` for its two `y`
  stores and addresses it by immediate everywhere else. Adding a sixth named
  pointer, or a block-scoped one narrowed to exactly those two stores, does
  produce the `addiu` but GCC then spills it (`sw` +1, `lw` +3) where retail
  keeps it in `$a0`;
- one `addu $fp, $v0, $zero`, retail's copy of `&poly[5]` into its saved
  register. Splitting the `r[1]` divide into a multiply and a divide recovers
  exactly this `addu`, at the cost of one extra `sw` for the intermediate, so
  it trades one difference for another and is not the answer;
- two `nop`. Retail leaves two load-delay slots unfilled that this build fills.

Crossed without moving the distance: all 32 two-temporary group patterns and a
greedy climb over the 18 per-block choices; every subset of the pointer set of
size 3-6; four placements for the pointer assignments (before the radii, after
each of the three, and immediately before the stores -- placement moves *where*
the setup lands, at instruction 68, 76, 80 or 92 against retail's 88, but never
the count); declaration order, which is inert; `&poly[n]` against `poly + n`
against an explicit byte cast, which GCC canonicalises to the same output;
`register` on any of the pointers; assigning `&poly[5]` through a temporary;
splitting each of the three divides and all eight combinations; and all 32
compiler profiles.

**A `__asm__("$30")` pin on the `&poly[5]` pointer reaches distance 3 and is a
miscompile.** GCC honours the pin and also leaves the scratch-pad base pointer
in `$s8`, so `addiu s8, s2, 180` overwrites it and every later
`lw v1, 0(s8)` reads the wrong address. Do not take that result; if a pin is
used here, disassemble and confirm every use of the register belongs to the one
variable.
