## `func_80042C08` at 0x80042C08

`gcc_2_8_1_g8`, 360 instructions against a target of 348, opcode multiset
distance 14. The first **87** instructions are exact, and every divergence
after that belongs to one class.

The gouraud "burst" builder. It lays eight `POLY_G4` into the PSX scratch pad
as four quadrant pairs on two rings, sweeps them through four angular steps of
`0x100`, and hands each quad to `func_8005B260` once per step. The quads start
degenerate -- every `y` at the object's centre -- and at the end of each step
the far edge (`x2`/`y2`, `x3`/`y3`) is promoted to the near edge (`x0`/`y0`,
`x1`/`y1`) with two word copies, so the eight quads trail the sweep.

Scratch pad layout, all three bases derived from one `lui` of `0x1F800000`:

| address | contents |
| --- | --- |
| `0x1F800000` | three `s32` working radii, reused by the `x` pass and the `y` pass |
| `0x1F800010` | the two `s16` scale factors off the object, widened to `s32` |
| `0x1F800020` | eight `POLY_G4`, `0x24` each |

Quad index bit 0 chooses the `x` sign, bit 1 the `y` sign and bit 2 the ring.
Quads 0..3 are the inner ring and take radii `r[0]`/`r[1]` with black outer
vertices; 4..7 are the outer ring, take `r[1]`/`r[2]` and are white throughout.
The three radii are `object->unk48`, `object->unk4A` and the constant `240`,
each scaled by `rcos`/`rsin` of the step and then by the matching factor, every
product rounded with the `bgez` / `addiu 0xFFF` / `sra 12` sequence that GCC
emits for `/ 4096`.

**Settled facts.** The profile is `-O2`: every `-O2` variant (`g8`, `g0`,
`_split`, `_no_split`) produces byte-identical output here and diverges first
at instruction 87, while `-O1` diverges at instruction 1 and `no_sched1` /
`no_sched2` at 1-4. The neighbours `func_80042B98` and `func_80042BC0` are
`gcc_2_8_1_g8`, which agrees.

Three facts got the prologue exact:

- *`cx` and `cy` are `s32` locals, not `s16`.* As `s16` they load with `lhu`
  and the target uses `lh`. Worth the whole `lh`/`lhu` column of the histogram.
- *The third radius reads `sc[0]` again instead of the hoisted copy.* The first
  two products share one load of `sc[0]`; the third reloads it. Writing all
  three against a hoisted `a` is one instruction short and moves the first
  divergence from 87 back to 80. So the source keeps `a = sc[0]` for the first
  two and spells the third `sc[0] * 240 / 4096`.
- *The stores are grouped by value, and that order is not a choice.* Retail's
  store order is `x0` for quads 1,3,5,7, then `x1` for the same four, then `x0`
  and `x1` for 0,2,4,6. Written that way, GCC's CSE produces retail's grouping
  (two stores for the first radius, four for the second, two for the third)
  with no further arranging.

**What is left is a single class: retail spills four quad base pointers and
reloads them 17 times, and that is what makes the coordinate stores interleave
with the loads.** Retail materialises `&poly[1]`, `&poly[7]`, `&poly[6]` and
`&poly[4]` into stack slots `0x1C`/`0x20`/`0x24`/`0x28`, keeps `&poly[5]` in
`$fp`, and rematerialises quads 0, 2 and 3 as `$s2 + imm`. Because the store
bases then come out of memory, the scheduler cannot disambiguate them from the
`lhu` reloads of `r[]` and is forced to interleave. Plain subscripting gives
GCC bases it *can* analyse, so it hoists all six `lhu` and their `addu` above
all sixteen `sh` and needs no spills at all -- 325 instructions, 23 short, with
zero reloads against retail's 17.

Naming the quads as pointer locals is the lever that reproduces the spills, but
no subset tried lands on 348:

| pointers named | instructions | aligned-equal | opcode distance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| none (subscripts) | 325 | 267 | 29 |
| 1, 7 | 338 | 285 | - |
| 1, 7, 5 | 350 | 288 | - |
| **1, 7, 5, 6** | **360** | **293** | **14** |
| 1, 7, 5, 6, 4 | 373 | 280 | - |
| all eight | 418 | 290 | - |

Placement was swept too -- before the radius block, after each of the three
radii, and immediately before the stores. Placement moves *where* the pointer
setup lands (instruction 68 when assigned early, 107 when assigned late,
against retail's 88) but never changes the count, so it is not the missing
piece. Retail computes them in the order 1, 7, 5, 6, 4, which is the order its
spill slots are assigned, and that order is already used above.

Also crossed without improving: writing the coordinate expressions inline
instead of through one reused `h` (394 instructions, GCC then materialises each
sum separately); a separate `u16 *` view of the scratch pad instead of
`*(u16 *)&r[n]` (347 instructions, but it rematerialises `lui 0x1f80` for the
`r[]` stores and pushes the first divergence back to 76); and `volatile` on the
radii, which is wrong on its own terms because retail reuses the register for
`r[n] = r[n] * sc[n] / 4096` rather than reloading.

Nothing here needs new linker aliases: the three scratch-pad bases are literal
addresses and `func_8005B260` is the only external symbol.
