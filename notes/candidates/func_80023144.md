## `func_80023144` at 0x80023144

`gcc_2_8_1_cc_g8_as_g4_split` plus `--use-comm-section`, **210 of 210
instructions, 25 differing**, and every one of the five call-delimited regions
is exactly the right length. Written from scratch; there was no previous
candidate.

The duel card panel setup. It indexes the 0x1C-byte record at `D_801A7AD8`,
clears `D_8009B34E` and `D_8009B355`, and for a card present (bit 0x8000 of
`+0x16`) reads the stats word for `id - 1`, publishes `gDuel_wSelectedCardID`,
and for a monster below level 0x14 latches an attribute nibble into
`D_8009B344`, adds 0x17 to it, and stores the `Duel_CalcCardStats` result as
two halfwords at `D_801D5608`. The slot's position within its row of fifteen
then selects the highlight state, and the two panel kinds at `+0x17` add their
own offsets before `TextBox_Create` and `func_80039A14`.

**No profile is added for this.** It needs the existing
`gcc_2_8_1_cc_g8_as_g4_split` with `--use-comm-section` appended to its maspsx
flags; adding an unused profile for an unmatched function is clutter. Both
parts are load-bearing - see below.

**Levers, in the order they paid.** Progression 206 -> 166 -> 149 -> 143 ->
64 -> 37 -> 33 -> 25.

- *A field read three times in the source is read once in the target.*
  `D_800E9FF0[..].f19` appears three times in the obvious spelling; hoisting it
  into a local is worth 40 positions.
- *`style` pinned to `$s2` so the `View *` parameter lands in `$s3`.*
- *`D_8009B1D5` and `D_8009B1C8` are gp-relative in the target, so this unit
  defines them.* Each use was otherwise costing an extra `lui`. The five
  `%hi`/`%lo` byte globals then need `section(".data")` to stay out of small
  data, or the count undershoots to 192.
- *The assembler needs `-G4`, not `-G1`.* `D_8009B1C8` is four bytes and the
  target reads it `lw $v0,704($gp)`. Without `--use-comm-section` the two owned
  globals land in `.sbss` with eight bytes genuinely allocated, which links at
  the wrong address while the object diff still looks clean.
- *`D_8009B344` is `volatile`.* The target stores the masked nibble, reads it
  back with `lbu`, adds 0x17 and stores again; every non-volatile spelling
  folds the read-back away.
- *The row test is `idx >= 15`, not `idx < 15`.* The target emits
  `slti`+`xori`; the other polarity drops the `xori`.
- *`idx % 15 >= 5` must be the first arm*, because the target's `slti` is
  followed by `bnez` and that arm is the fall-through.
- *The subscript must be spelt `*(s32 *)((s32)sb + ((id - 1) << 2))`* to force
  the separate `addiu $v0,$a0,-1`; `sb[id - 1]` folds the -1 into the load
  displacement. This and moving `sb = gDuel_adwCardStats` inside the `if` are
  **only correct together** - each alone changes the count the wrong way.
- *Pin the `Duel_CalcCardStats` result to `$5`.* The target copies it out of
  `$v0` so `$v0` stays free as the temporary for both halves. Worth 120
  positions on its own, because it was the last length difference and the whole
  stream re-aligned.
- *Give the later-used array a pointer local, assigned before the other chain
  is touched* - including above the `style += 4` that sat between them. Worth
  eight. Found by reducing the block to a ten-line probe rather than by
  permuting the real source.

**What is left is 25 positions of register naming and two orderings**, on an
exact instruction multiset with exact region lengths:

- `move $a0,$s0` sits in the call's branch delay slot in the target and in the
  preceding load-delay gap in the build.
- `r >> 16` computes into `$v0` in the target and into `$a1` in the build,
  clobbering the pinned `r`, which is dead by then so GCC reuses it. A probe
  reproduces this exactly and no spelling tried moves it: separate `lo`/`hi`
  locals, reversing the two stores, an explicit `(s32)` cast, and a pointer
  local for the destination all keep `sra $5,$5,16`.
- The `addiu $a0,$a0,%lo(D_801D5608)` is emitted adjacent to its `lui` rather
  than deferred to the use; the array-name spelling does not defer it either.
- Both pins remain load-bearing: the unpin sweep over every subset finds
  nothing better, and pinning `kind` to `$4`, `$5` or `$2` does not help.
