## `func_80023144` at 0x80023144

`gcc_2_8_1_cc_g8_as_g4_split` plus `--use-comm-section`, **210 of 210
instructions, 25 differing**, and every one of the five call-delimited regions
is exactly the right length. Written from scratch; there was no previous
candidate.

The duel card panel setup. It indexes the 0x1C-byte record at `D_801A7AD8`,
clears `D_8009B34E` and `D_8009B355`, and for a card present (bit 0x8000 of
`+0x16`) reads the stats word for `id - 1`, publishes `gDuel_wSelectedCardID`,
and for a monster below level 0x14 latches an attribute nibble into
`D_8009B344`, adds 0x17 to it, and stores the `Duel_CalcCardStats` result as
two halfwords at `D_801D5608`. The slot's position within its row of fifteen
then selects the highlight state, and the two panel kinds at `+0x17` add their
own offsets before `TextBox_Create` and `func_80039A14`.

**No profile is added for this.** It needs the existing
`gcc_2_8_1_cc_g8_as_g4_split` with `--use-comm-section` appended to its maspsx
flags; adding an unused profile for an unmatched function is clutter. Both
parts are load-bearing - see below.

**Levers, in the order they paid.** Progression 206 -> 166 -> 149 -> 143 ->
64 -> 37 -> 33 -> 25.

  `D_800E9FF0[..].f19` appears three times in the obvious spelling; hoisting it
  into a local is worth 40 positions.

**The matched sibling `func_80060E70` is the reference for this shape.** It
touches `gDuel_adwCardStats`, `D_801D5608`, `gDuel_wSelectedCardID` and
`D_801A8000`, and reading it after reaching 25 supplied the last two levers
directly:

  pointer local.* The sibling writes `D_801D5608[0] = id; dst[1] = q[0];`. That
  mixed spelling is what produces `sw v0,%lo(D_801D5608)(v1)` followed by

  first `D_801D5608` site. The pointer local must be assigned immediately
  before the call; assigning it at the top of the function costs 180 positions.
- *The sibling pins three ordinary locals*, which is what prompted re-reading
  the register assignment at the two later `D_801D5608` sites rather than

**A column-order error cost several cycles here and is worth recording.** In
`fndiff.py` the left column is the *build* and the right is the *target*; the
relocation tag is printed after the right column but belongs to the build.
Reading it the other way round produced a confident and exactly inverted
conclusion - `kind` in `a0` and the pointer in `a1` - and the pins derived from
it moved the count the wrong way. Pinning the pointer to `$4` rather than `$5`,
once the columns were read correctly, took the count from 23 to 11 in one step.
**When a pin derived from a diff makes things worse, check the diff's column
order before trying a different register.**

**Eleven positions remain, in three clusters**, on an exact instruction
multiset with exact region lengths:

- *Two positions, argument move.* GCC's `sched1` hoists `move $4,$16` into the
  load-delay gap between `lbu $2,D_8009B344` and `addu $2,$2,23`; the target
  leaves that gap empty and spends the move in the `jal` delay slot. This is
  visible in GCC's own assembly before MASPSX runs, so it is a scheduling
  decision and not a load-delay artefact. `-fno-schedule-insns` is not the
  answer: it costs 193 positions, so `sched1` is certainly enabled in the
  target. Restating the volatile update four ways, passing the argument as
  `&D_801A7AD8[idx]`, and assigning the pointer local in the gap all leave it
  unchanged.
- *Two positions, shift temporary.* `r >> 16` computes into `$a1` in the build
  and `$v0` in the target. Both registers are dead at that point, so this is an
  allocator tie-break. Eight spellings of the two stores - explicit `(s32)`
  cast, a block-scoped temporary, `| 0`, `& 0xFFFF`, division by 65536, a
  `(s16 *)` store - all give exactly 11, so it is invariant to source spelling.
  Pinning an explicit high-half local to `$2` costs three or four positions
  because the copy stops being free.
- *Seven positions, deferred `addiu`.* At the two later `D_801D5608` sites the
  target emits `lui a0,%hi` early and defers `addiu a0,a0,%lo` to just before
  the use; the build emits the pair adjacently. Hoisting the pointer local to a
  single assignment - the sibling's shape - costs 22 to 187 depending on
  placement, and dropping it in favour of the array name costs 22.

`-fno-strength-reduce` is neutral here at 11, so the sibling's
`no_strength_reduce` profile is not the discriminator.
