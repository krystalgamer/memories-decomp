## `func_80023144` at 0x80023144

`gcc_2_8_1_g8_split`, 210 of 210 instructions, **7 differing positions**
with the two pins below, 29 without them. The whole instruction sequence is retail's; what is
left is register allocation in the two duelist-rank blocks, one
allocation in the card-stats lookup, and one delay slot that follows
from it.

The card detail panel behind a field cursor, called by `func_8002348C`
with the grid slot the cursor is over. It clears the two panel state
bytes, and if the record at that slot is occupied (`flags & 0x8000`)
publishes the card: `gDuel_wSelectedCardID`, `D_8009B34E = 1`, and for a
card whose `gDuel_adwCardStats` type digit is below 0x14 the guardian
star digit at `D_8009B344` (bits 18..21 when `flags & 0x200`, else bits
22..25, then biased by 0x17) plus the attack and defence halves of
`Duel_CalcCardStats` into `D_801D5608[0]` and `[1]`; a type at or above
0x14 selects panel style 0x51 instead of 0x50. The slot's position on

when `flags & 0x1000`, and back to 2 when the slot's side matches
`D_8009B1D5` and `D_8009B1C8[0x1F]` is clear; a monster-row slot with

both clamping a value outside 0..3 to 3 and setting bit 0 of

panel is then created at the cursor object's +0x30/+0x32 position.

**What the shape needed.**

- *`D_8009B34E`, `D_8009B355`, `D_8009B344`, `D_8009B320` and
  `gDuel_wSelectedCardID` are declared as scalars in `.data`, not as
  arrays.* All five are one or two bytes and would otherwise be
  small-data at `-G8`, which is what `D_8009B1D5` and `D_8009B1C8` are:
  retail reaches those two through `$gp` and the other five through the
  assembler-macro `lui $at` / `%lo` form. Declared `extern u8 X[]`
  instead, GCC materialises one base register and shares it across all
  the accesses, which costs the `$at` form and two instructions.
- *The array base goes into a local before the index arithmetic.*
  `table = gDuel_adwCardStats;` then `table[value]` keeps the `-1` on
  the index (`addiu $v0, $a0, -0x1` / `sll` / `addu`); writing
  `gDuel_adwCardStats[id - 1]` lets `fold` distribute the multiply and
  the offset collapses into the load as `lw ..., -4(...)`.
- *The `>= 5` arm of the row test comes first.* Retail branches to the
  monster-row arm and falls through to the other one, so the source has
  to read `if (index % 15 >= 5) { ... } else { ... }`.
- *The unsigned compares are `>= 0 &&` conjuncts.* `sltiu $v0, $v0, 0x4`
  on the `pinned` byte and `sltiu` on the type digit are `fold` merging
  a `>= 0` test into the bound, not an unsigned variable.
- *One function-scope variable carries the `id - 1` index, the
  `Duel_CalcCardStats` result and the `index >= 15` flag.* Their live
  ranges are disjoint and retail allocates them to one register. A
  block-local variable for the call result is coalesced with `$v0` and
  the `addu $a1, $v0, $zero` copy disappears, which costs the
  instruction count; a second function-scope variable for the flag
  alone allocates the pair the other way round.
- *`rank` and `side` are block-local in each of the two rank blocks.*

**Where the residual is: two clusters, and they are coupled.**

The stored source carries the two pins that the previous entry described but
left out: `register s32 rank asm("$2")` in both rank blocks and
`register s32 *dst asm("$4") = D_801D5608;` alongside it. They take the
candidate from 29 to **7**, and the neighbouring `func_80023090` in this same
translation unit is a matched function with four accepted pins, so a pinned
finish is house-legal here.

*Two positions, the `id - 1` temporary.* Retail computes it into `$v0`
(`addiu v0,a0,-1` / `sll v0,v0,2`); the candidate uses `$a1`
(`addiu a1,a0,-1` / `sll v0,a1,2`), because the one function-scope `value`
variable that carries the index, the `Duel_CalcCardStats` result and the
`index >= 15` flag is allocated to `$a1` for its whole range.

*Five positions, the `D_801D5608` address.* In both rank blocks the candidate
starts the `lui`/`addiu` pair exactly one delay slot earlier than retail. The
three positions in the second block make this precise: retail leaves the
`beqz` delay slot at +0x27C empty, puts `lui a0,%hi` in the *next* branch delay
slot at +0x290, and spends `addiu a0,a0,%lo` in the load-delay slot after
`lb v0,25(v0)` at +0x2AC. The candidate does each one slot sooner and ends with
the spare `nop` at the end instead of the start.

**The two clusters are mutually exclusive, which is the new measurement.**
Giving the call result its own variable pinned to `$5`, and leaving `value` to
carry only the index and the flag, *fixes the `id - 1` cluster completely* -
and costs four positions elsewhere, for a net 9. Two of those are the shift
temporary: the pinned result is dead after the second store, so GCC reuses
`$a1` for `value >> 16` where retail uses `$v0`. The other two are an `xori`
that moves to `$a1` for the same reason. Splitting the index into its own
variable instead, with or without pinning it to `$2` or pinning `table` to
`$3`, costs 13. Pinning `value` itself to `$2` costs 157, because the copy
`move a1,v0` that the shared variable produces disappears and the instruction
count changes.

So the shared variable is what produces retail's copy, and it is also what puts
the index in the wrong register. Any fix has to keep the copy while freeing the
index, and no spelling tried does both.

**Ruled out for the address cluster.** Deferring the pointer assignment out of
the initialised declaration to just before its use, in two placements, costs 14.
Initialising `side` or `rank` in their declarations ahead of the pointer is
neutral. The mixed spelling that worked on the matched sibling `func_80060E70`
- first store through the array name, second through the pointer - is neutral
in both directions here, and dropping the pointer for the array name costs 11.

**Ruled out globally, measured.** Twelve GCC options one at a time:
`-fno-cse-follow-jumps`, `-fno-peephole`, `-fno-force-mem`, `-fno-caller-saves`,
`-fno-function-cse`, `-frerun-cse-after-loop`, `-fthread-jumps`, `-fno-inline`,
`-fomit-frame-pointer` and `-fno-strength-reduce` are neutral;
`-fno-expensive-optimizations` and `-fno-cse-skip-blocks` cost a few, and
`-fno-delayed-branch` 216. `-fno-schedule-insns` costs 193, so `sched1` is
certainly enabled. `-mno-split-addresses` costs 87.

**Two instrument notes from this campaign.**

- *The matched sibling is the best reference available.* `func_80060E70` at
  0x80060E70 touches `gDuel_adwCardStats`, `D_801D5608`,
  `gDuel_wSelectedCardID` and `D_801A8000`, and reading it supplied the mixed
  array-name/pointer store spelling directly. **Check the matched neighbours
  and matched users of the same globals before treating a shape as unknown.**
- *In `fndiff.py` the left column is the build and the right is the target*,
  and the relocation tag prints after the right column but belongs to the
  build. Reading it the other way round produces a confident and exactly
  inverted conclusion; a pin derived from the reversed reading moved the count
  the wrong way, and correcting it took an earlier line of this campaign from
  23 to 11 in one step. **When a pin derived from a diff makes things worse,
  check the diff's column order before trying a different register.**
- *Check what profile the adjacent matched functions use before adding one.*
  An earlier line here carried a locally added
  `gcc_2_8_1_cc_g8_as_g4_split` plus `--use-comm-section`; the matched
  immediate neighbour `func_80022D94` uses `gcc_2_8_1_g8_split`, and the
  tracked profile turned out to reproduce the same result, so the addition was
  discarded.
