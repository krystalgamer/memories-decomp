## `func_80023144` at 0x80023144

`gcc_2_8_1_g8_split`, 210 of 210 instructions, opcode distance 2, 29
differing positions. The whole instruction sequence is retail's; what is
left is register allocation in the two duelist-rank blocks, one
allocation in the card-stats lookup, and one delay slot that follows
from it.

The card detail panel behind a field cursor, called by `func_8002348C`
with the grid slot the cursor is over. It clears the two panel state
bytes, and if the record at that slot is occupied (`flags & 0x8000`)
publishes the card: `gDuel_wSelectedCardID`, `D_8009B34E = 1`, and for a
card whose `gDuel_adwCardStats` type digit is below 0x14 the guardian
star digit at `D_8009B344` (bits 18..21 when `flags & 0x200`, else bits
22..25, then biased by 0x17) plus the attack and defence halves of
`Duel_CalcCardStats` into `D_801D5608[0]` and `[1]`; a type at or above
0x14 selects panel style 0x51 instead of 0x50. The slot's position on
its side then adjusts `D_8009B34E`: the two non-monster rows get 2, or 3
when `flags & 0x1000`, and back to 2 when the slot's side matches
`D_8009B1D5` and `D_8009B1C8[0x1F]` is clear; a monster-row slot with
`flags & 0x2000` clears it. Cursor kind 3 adds 4 to the style and
records the opposing duelist's `pinned` byte, kind 2 with the flag at
+0x18 adds 2 and records the byte for the side the cursor's row selects,
both clamping a value outside 0..3 to 3 and setting bit 0 of
`D_8009B355`. Kind 3 also or-s in bit 1 and runs the guardian-star
comparison `func_80023090` against the side's selection record. The
panel is then created at the cursor object's +0x30/+0x32 position.

**What the shape needed.**

- *`D_8009B34E`, `D_8009B355`, `D_8009B344`, `D_8009B320` and
  `gDuel_wSelectedCardID` are declared as scalars in `.data`, not as
  arrays.* All five are one or two bytes and would otherwise be
  small-data at `-G8`, which is what `D_8009B1D5` and `D_8009B1C8` are:
  retail reaches those two through `$gp` and the other five through the
  assembler-macro `lui $at` / `%lo` form. Declared `extern u8 X[]`
  instead, GCC materialises one base register and shares it across all
  the accesses, which costs the `$at` form and two instructions.
- *The array base goes into a local before the index arithmetic.*
  `table = gDuel_adwCardStats;` then `table[value]` keeps the `-1` on
  the index (`addiu $v0, $a0, -0x1` / `sll` / `addu`); writing
  `gDuel_adwCardStats[id - 1]` lets `fold` distribute the multiply and
  the offset collapses into the load as `lw ..., -4(...)`.
- *The `>= 5` arm of the row test comes first.* Retail branches to the
  monster-row arm and falls through to the other one, so the source has
  to read `if (index % 15 >= 5) { ... } else { ... }`.
- *The unsigned compares are `>= 0 &&` conjuncts.* `sltiu $v0, $v0, 0x4`
  on the `pinned` byte and `sltiu` on the type digit are `fold` merging
  a `>= 0` test into the bound, not an unsigned variable.
- *One function-scope variable carries the `id - 1` index, the
  `Duel_CalcCardStats` result and the `index >= 15` flag.* Their live
  ranges are disjoint and retail allocates them to one register. A
  block-local variable for the call result is coalesced with `$v0` and
  the `addu $a1, $v0, $zero` copy disappears, which costs the
  instruction count; a second function-scope variable for the flag
  alone allocates the pair the other way round.
- *`rank` and `side` are block-local in each of the two rank blocks.*

**Where the residual is.** Two independent things.

`D_800E9FF0`'s base and the `pinned` chain take the opposite registers
from retail in both rank blocks: retail keeps the chain
(`lbu`, `xori`, `sll`, `addu`, `lb`) in `$v0` with the base in `$v1`,
the candidate keeps the base in `$v0` and the chain in `$v1`. With that
swapped the `D_801D5608` base follows, and so does which of the two
`%hi` halves the delay-slot filler takes: retail hoists
`lui $a0, %hi(D_801D5608)` to the head of the block and leaves its
`%lo` to fill the load-delay slot after `lb`, the candidate hoists
`lui %hi(D_800E9FF0)` instead. This is the whole of the 16 positions
between 0x800232FC and 0x80023414 and is not reachable from source
shape: an index temporary, a pointer to the entry, a raw byte access, a
block-local `D_801D5608` pointer, the same pointer assigned before or
after the rank read, one at function scope, and the chained
`D_801D5608[2] = rank = ...` spelling were all measured and none of them
moved it.

Pinning does move it, and cheaply, which is the evidence that nothing
else is wrong: `register s32 rank asm("$2")` in both blocks together
with `register s32 *dst asm("$4") = D_801D5608;` takes the candidate to
7 differing positions. Adding a pin for `side` as well makes it worse
(12), so the second block wants `side` allocated freely. The 7 that
remain are the two `value` positions below plus the same `%lo`
placement in both blocks. Retail's own neighbour in this translation
unit, `func_80023090`, is a matched function with four accepted pins, so
a pinned finish is house-legal here; it was left out of the stored
source because the unpinned form is the honest measurement.

The other two positions are the `id - 1` temporary: retail computes it
into `$v0`, and sharing the variable with the call result (which is what
produces the `addu $a1, $v0, $zero` copy retail has) puts it in `$a1`.
Moving the shared role to `stats`, `id`, `rank` or a fresh variable
fixes those two and costs four elsewhere, because the pre-call
`gDuel_adwCardStats` value then leaves `$v1`.
