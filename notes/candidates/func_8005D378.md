This is **not** a near miss - it is 5 instructions long and recorded so the

**What the function is.** A keyframe interpolator for one animation node. It
takes the animation context, indexes a pointer array at `+0x14` by the context's
own `+0x14` field, and reads four slots from there: a track record and two
sample records, plus an optional publish pointer. The track's `+0x12` is the
duration and `+0x10` the elapsed time; a zero duration returns 1 immediately.

Every value is the same weighted average, `(from * t + to * (duration - t)) /
duration`, applied to nine quantities: the three translation components at
`+0x0`/`+0x2`/`+0x4` of each sample, the three angles at `+0x6`/`+0x8`/`+0xA`,
and the three scale components at `+0xC`/`+0xE`/`+0x10`.

The destination node is found by unpacking the track's first word: the top byte
indexes the same pointer array and the low 24 bits are a word index into it.
Translation lands at `+0x18`/`+0x1C`/`+0x20`, the interpolated angles at
`+0x44`/`+0x46`/`+0x48`, and `RotMatrixYXZ_gte` turns those into a matrix at
`+0x4`, which `ScaleMatrix` then scales. `+0x0` is cleared, and if the fourth
slot is non-null the three translation components are published to it.

**Angle wrapping, and it is the same idiom as `func_8004E7B0`.** When the
duration is exactly 16, each angle pair is put on the shortest path first: if
the two differ by `0x801` or more in either direction, the *destination* angle
is moved a full turn of `0x1000` toward the other, `+` when it is at or below
zero and `-` otherwise. Two details are easy to get backwards and both cost
instructions: it is the **`to`** angle that moves, not the `from` angle, and the
wrap is skipped when the duration is anything other than 16.

**Four structural findings so far.**

- *The two sample pointers are re-read from the slot array for every component,
  not cached in locals.* Caching them costs 15 instructions and 16 positions.
  The same applies to the publish pointer, which is re-read for each of its
  three stores.
- *The six angles, by contrast, are read into locals together* before any of
  them is used. That is worth 29 aligned instructions over reading them
  individually, so the rule is not "never cache" but "cache what retail loads in
  one run".
- *The publish stores read the low halfword of the translation*, which is a
  32-bit field, so they are `lhu` of `*(u16 *)&node->x` rather than a narrowing
  of the `s32`.
- *The three wrap bodies must stay separate.* Writing them with `goto` so they
  chain lets GCC cross-jump the identical tails and loses 11 more instructions;
  the `if`/`else if` form keeps them apart.

Writing it `(u8 *)ctx + ((index << 2) + 0x14)` - the same value, one pair of

instructions. `index * 4 + 0x14` and hoisting the offset into its own local are
identical to it.

It is worth being precise about why, because "add brackets" is not a lever.
The two expressions differ in *association*, and association decides whether
the constant joins the index or the base. Joined to the index it becomes part
of one offset that is computed once; joined to the base it is a separate
addend that GCC can sink into each load's displacement. Any candidate whose
target keeps a base pointer while the build repeats a displacement is worth
checking for this.

**And it has an answer: assign the destination inside each arm.** Writing

```c
w = (s16)rx;
if (w <= 0) { rx = w + MODEL_ANGLE_FULL_TURN; }
else        { rx = w - MODEL_ANGLE_FULL_TURN; }
```

instead of adjusting `w` in both arms and storing it once afterwards **removes
the `blez`/`bgtz` mismatch entirely**. With one destination the two arms differ
only in a constant, so GCC folds them - it can put one adjustment in the branch
delay slot and correct in the other arm - and three instructions suffice. With
the destination written in each arm they are two separate assignments and the
long form is forced.

The measurement is mixed and is recorded as such: 256 differing and 4
instructions short, against the stored form's 263 and 5 long, but 350 aligned
against 362. The inner branch shape is right and something else has moved out
of place, so it is not adopted yet. What remains in that variant is the outer
test only - three `beqz` and three `j` in the target against three `bnez` and
three `bgez` here - which is a smaller and better-understood residual than the
stored form's.

The general shape is worth remembering: **a two-armed adjustment that writes one
variable is not the same as one that writes the variable twice**, even though
the two are identical in meaning. The first invites GCC to fold the arms; the
second forbids it.
