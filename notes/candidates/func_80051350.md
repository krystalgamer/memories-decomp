**The vector block is simply absent.** The target has four `break`, so two
checked divisions; this draft has one. It has six more `mult` and seven more
`mflo`, which is exactly the block between `0x80051868` and `0x80051930` -
`F[3]*F[2]`, `s1*s1`, `s0*s0`, `F[0]*F[5]`, `s2*s1`, `s3*s0`, a third
`SquareRoot0`, and a division by its result. That is a 2D cross product divided
by a segment length, so a signed distance from a line, feeding the `D_8009AF98`
countdown and the `D_8009AF99` sign byte. Writing it should recover most of the
`mult`, `mflo` and `break` deficit and a good share of the loads.

**The rest is spilling.** Even allowing for the missing block, `lw -47` and
`sw -20` are far larger than it can explain. The target commits ten
callee-saved registers and keeps its intermediates on the stack; this draft
holds them in locals that GCC is free to keep in registers. That is the same
pressure difference recorded on `func_80044838`, and it is the next thing to
work on after the vector block.

**The addressing check came back negative, which is worth recording.** The entry
flagged `lui` as the first thing to test, because two globals are `%hi`/`%lo`
while three are `%gp_rel`. `lui` does not appear in the top twelve differences
at all, so declaring `D_800F2C40` and `D_800F56F0` as plain arrays already
produces the target's addressing. The check cost one command and removed a whole
class of causes.
