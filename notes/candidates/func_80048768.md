A sound-driver pan update. The first argument is a key: bit 0x8000 forwards
straight to `func_80044E90` with the pan sign-extended, and the `0xF000 ==
0x4000` form is an indirect key that is resolved through two tables hanging off

call. The main pass walks the four voices, reads each envelope through
`SpuGetVoiceEnvelope`, and for the voice whose key at +0x404 matches, scales the
sample's base level at +0x444 by the distance either side of centre and stores
the two channel levels at +0x414 and +0x41C.

neighbours: a `u8 *` reloaded from the global and indexed with byte
displacements, which is what produces the `lw` of the pointer followed by
`0x404`, `0x414`, `0x41C`, `0x43C`, `0x444` and `0x44C` offsets rather than a
struct layout.

Two shape facts are already visible.

**The near tests are unsigned window checks, not comparisons.** `(u16)(pan - 1)
< 0x80` and `(u16)(pan + 0x80) < 0x80` compile to the `andi 0xFFFF` plus
`sltiu 0x80` pairs the target uses; writing them as signed range tests produces
compares instead.

**The left scale is hoisted out of the voice loop but the right one is not.**
The target computes `0x80 - pan` and `pan + 0x80` once before the loop, then
reloads `g_SDValue` separately inside the right-hand arm, which is why that arm
carries its own `lui`/`lw` pair.
