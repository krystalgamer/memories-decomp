## Four things the shape needed

**The magic constant is a divide by five, not ten.** `0x66666667` with
`sra $a0, $t1, 1` after the `mfhi` is `x / 5`; `x / 10` is the same multiplier
with `sra 2`. Every one of the five action arguments is `(s8)record->
table_index % 5` plus a literal (`+ 6`, `+ 1`, `+ 0x38`). Reading it as `% 10`
costs nine instructions across the function and was the single biggest error in
the first draft. The one that really is a different divisor is the fallback at
`.L80027CCC`, which uses `0x88888889` with `addu` then `sra 3` - that one is
`% 15`, and its result is re-narrowed with `(s8)` before the `- 4`.

**The three stack arrays are six pointers each and the collector
null-terminates.** The frame is `0x70`: `0x10` of outgoing arguments, then the
opponent-scan array at `0x10`, the hand list at `0x28` and the field list at
`0x40`, then five saves from `0x58`. `Duel_CollectFieldCardsByType` writes
`DuelCardRecord *` and stores a terminating zero, which is what both list walks
test.

**The hand walk has to be a `goto` loop.** As a `do`/`while` GCC hoists the
`0x90000000` mask out of the *outer* loop as well as the opponent scan, which
needs a fifth callee-saved register and costs four instructions; retail
materialises it inside, immediately before the scan. The `goto` form stops
that. It does cost the loop's own invariant motion, which is the residual
below.

**The block order is `[pick] [found] [no-equip] [walk]`, and it needs three
gotos.** Retail places the equip-found body *after* the random-pick body and
before the hand walk, and reaches the walk's `slot = 0xA` by falling out of the
failed collector test. Written naturally - the found body inline in the inner
loop and the pick body in an `if (n != 0)` - the layout is different in three
places and scores 205 rather than 176.

## What is left

Eight opcodes: the build has two `lui` and one `beqz` the target does not, and
is missing one `bne`, one `addu`, one `sra` and two `sll`-class instructions
(two of which are the `nop`s the target's schedule needs and this one does
not).

The largest identified cause is that the `goto` loop stops GCC hoisting the
four loop-invariant bases - `D_800907D8`, `D_800EAE88`, `D_801A7AD8` and
`D_801A7AD8 + 0x8C` - which retail sets up in `$a1`, `$s1`, `$a0` and `$s2`
before the walk. Hoisting them by hand in the source does put them there, and
takes the opcode distance from 14 to 8, but GCC then gives one of the two that
retail keeps caller-saved a fifth callee-saved register, so the build runs two
instructions short and the prologue gains a save. The three placements of
`slot = 0xA` were swept against that: before the `rand` test (271
instructions, distance 12 to 14), on both paths with an `if`/`else`
(269/8), and with a `goto` out of the early path (269/8, the form stored here).
Nothing found so far gets the count and the hoist at the same time, and that is
the question to start from.
