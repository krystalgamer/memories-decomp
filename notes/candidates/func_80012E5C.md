**Levers, in the order they paid.**

- *Seven globals are gp-relative and therefore defined, not declared.*
  `D_8009B0A8`, `D_8009B0AD`, `D_8009B0D0`, `D_8009B098`, `D_8009B0A0`,
  `gGraphics_bActiveBuffer` and `D_8009B0B4` all appear as `%gp_rel` in the
  target, which only happens when the translation unit defines them, so they
  are definitions here and the profile carries `--use-comm-section`. The
  offsets check out against `gp = 0x8009AF08`: 400, 408, 416, 420, 421, 428 and
  456 respectively.
- *The byte scalars that are only referenced need pushing out of small data.*
  `D_8009B141` through `D_8009B144`, `D_8009B318` and `D_8009AFA2` are one byte
  each, so `-G8` would put them in small data and address them off `$gp`; the
  target uses `%hi`/`%lo`, so each carries `section(".data")`. Worth 72
  positions on its own, 128 down to 56.
- *The buffer index is read back from the global it was just stored in.*
  The target stores the `func_80085320` result to `gGraphics_bActiveBuffer` and
  then computes `andi $v1,$v0,0xff` from the same register. Writing the two
  bank subscripts as `gGraphics_bActiveBuffer * N` rather than
  `(idx & 0xFF) * N` produces exactly that: the store has to precede the read,
  and CSE turns the read into a truncation of the stored value instead of a
  reload. 44 down to 28.

  folds the displacement into the relocation and builds two instructions where
  the target has three. Assigning `base = D_800E9D90` first and then
  `slot = base + 3` restores `lui`/`addiu`/`addiu` and is what finally brought
  the instruction count to 132 against 132. 28 down to 15, and pinning `base`
  to `$2` took it to 12.
- *`func_80085DB0` takes the slot pointer as a third argument.* The target
  computes the record pointer into `$a2` and passes it, which is why `$a2`
  rather than a scratch register holds it across `sw $a2,0($s0)` and the
  delay-slot `sw $v0,0($a2)`. Declaring the callee as taking three arguments
  and passing `ptr` is worth four positions on its own, 12 down to 8.
- *Index the table by its array name, not through a pointer variable.*
  `D_8009B0A0[i]` emits `addu $v0,$s1,$s3`, index before base, which is the
  target's operand order; a `u8 *` local holding the same address emits
  `addu $v0,$s3,$s1`. Casting the base to `s32` and adding integers does not
  change this either way.

**What is left** is eight positions in two clusters, both orderings within a
basic block rather than anything structural, on an exact instruction multiset.

- Three positions where the target materialises `&D_8009B0A0` *before* storing
  `gGraphics_bActiveBuffer`, and the build emits the store, the `andi` and then
  the address. GCC always emits `la $19,D_8009B0A0` after the `andi`, for every
  source position of the assignment tried -- before the swap call, immediately
  after it, and not at all with the address left to loop-invariant motion --
  and for `-fno-schedule-insns`, `-fno-schedule-insns2`,
  `-fno-expensive-optimizations`, `-fno-strength-reduce`, `-fno-delayed-branch`,
  `-fno-cse-follow-jumps` and `-fno-thread-jumps`.
- Five positions of loop rotation. The target's loop carries the zero
  argument, emitting `move $a0,$zero` in the `bgez` delay slot and
  `move $a1,$a0` at the top; the build rotates the index address instead,
  putting `addu $v0,$s1,$s3` in the delay slot and spreading the two argument
  moves. Spelling the zero as a variable assigned before the loop makes GCC
  allocate it a register and costs three extra instructions. Moving the
  decrement after the call, `while` instead of `do`, and reordering the store
  and the byte read within the body all score 9 to 25.
