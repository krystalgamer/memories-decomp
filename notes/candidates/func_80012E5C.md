## What the shape needed

**No `volatile` on the republished bytes.** The neighbouring `func_80013154`
declares its init block `volatile` so a run of constant stores keeps source
order. Here the five values are *loads*, and retail batches all five loads
before all five stores. `volatile` forces load/store pairs and costs eight
`nop`s.

**`D_8009B318` and `D_8009B141` are outside small data.** Both are read twice
through `lui`/`%lo`; declared as plain externs under `_comm` they become
`gp`-relative and the function builds four instructions short. `D_8009AFA2` is
the same, and its store is the assembler `$at` macro form.

**The tail loop is a `do`/`while` over a descending counter with the two
cursors held in registers,** the ordering-table base re-read from
`D_8009B0B4` each iteration because the call may move it.

**`off -= 0x14` and `k--` must both precede the call.** This is what decides
the back-branch delay slot. With either one after the call, reorg has a spare
`addiu` to steal and takes it; with both before, the only candidate left is the
`move $a0, $zero` that re-zeroes the first argument after the call clobbers it,
which is what retail has. That one change is worth three positions.

**The two zero arguments are literals, not a variable.** A `register` local
pinned to `$4` also produces the peeled zero, but GCC then treats the pinned
caller-saved register as surviving the call and re-truncates it with
`andi $a0, $a0, 0xffff` instead of re-zeroing.
