function is now byte-exact, including the two `sw` stores, the `move $a0, $a1`
fifth read, the four `sh` stores and the reload sequence. The whole residual is
in the head, and it is one scheduling decision.

The fifth-read finding from the previous entry stands unchanged and is what
makes the tail reachable: the `move $a0, $a1` is an ordinary read of
`c->music_track` that GCC forwards from the value it has just stored, not a
copy to be defeated.

**Two further levers got it from 28 to 16, and both are about live ranges
rather than statement order.**

*One local per reload, not a reused pair.* The previous entry reused `p` for
the `0x801EA800` constant and for three of the four `music_track` reloads, and
`q` for the other two. That is one pseudo carrying five disjoint ranges, and
the allocator splits it differently from five pseudos with one range each.
Giving the constant its own local and each reload its own local is worth
28 -> 18 on `no_sched2`. No emitted instruction changes; only which register
each lands in.

*The first two `g_SDValue` reads are one variable.* The first pointer is dead
before the second read -- its last use is the `0x158A` store, and `0x1582` is
written through the *second* pointer. Retail reuses `$v0` for both. Two locals
`a` and `b` give the allocator two pseudos and it takes a fresh register for
the second; writing both as `a` reuses one. With the change above this is
18 -> 16, and it flips the better profile from `no_sched2` back to plain
`gcc_2_8_1_g0`: once the live ranges are right, the second scheduler produces
retail's placement of `li $v0, -1` and `move $a0, $a1` above the `sh` block
rather than below it.

**What is left is which instruction fills one load-delay slot.** Retail emits

```
lui   $v0, %hi(g_SDValue)
lw    $v0, %lo(g_SDValue)($v0)
lui   $a0, 0x801E              # fills the load delay
sh    $zero, 0x1586($v0)
```

and this build emits `lui $a0, 0x801E` *before* the load and fills the delay
with `li $v0, 255` instead. That one choice extends the `255` live range across
the first pointer's three stores, so `255` takes `$v0`, the shared `g_SDValue`
pointer is pushed to `$a2` and `0x801E2000` to `$a1`. The 16 positions are that
single register shift, not 16 independent problems.

Crossed without improving on 16: both constants introduced as named locals at
every statement position from the top of the function down to their use (22,
and the position within that sweep does not matter -- the back end places the
`lui` regardless); `0xFF` as a named local at every position (also 22, same
reason); the second and third `g_SDValue` reads made `volatile` to stop the
load moving across the stores, alone, together, and with all three volatile
(26, 27, and an instruction-count change); and all viable profiles, where only
`gcc_2_8_1_g0` reaches 16 and `no_sched2` reaches 19.

The next attempt should aim at the delay-slot filler directly. Statement
position provably does not move either `lui`, so the lever wanted is whatever
changes the scheduler's priority between `lui $a0, 0x801E` and `li 255` at
that point -- not where the constant is written.
