**What the shape needed.**

- *The sign bit of the opponent id must be computed before the object is
  created.* Written inline as the sixth argument of the second `func_800404CC`
  it is materialised in `$v0` at the call, and the function then builds one
  callee-saved register short: the object pointer takes `$s1`, the constant
  `11` takes `$s0`, and `$s2` is never allocated. Assigning it to its own local
  above `obj = func_800400AC(...)` forces it live across two calls, which puts
  it in `$s0` and moves the other two up to retail's `$s1`/`$s2`. Worth the
  whole prologue and 89 positions.
- *`D_800EA0E8` is walked from its own base.* `*(s16 *)(D_800EA0E8 + 40)` folds
  the `40` into the relocation and re-bases the register; a local `pane` set to
  the array gives retail's `40($s0)` .. `110($s0)` displacements.
- *`gDuel_bTerrain` takes the assembler macro form, not split addressing.*
  Retail's two reads are each an adjacent `lui`/`lbu` pair, which an array
  spelling under `-msplit-addresses` breaks apart: the `%hi` becomes its own
  pseudo and is emitted before the callback address rather than after. Declared
  as a scalar with `section(".data")` the pair stays adjacent. Worth 5 words,
  and it is the only symbol in the function that wants that form -- the same
  test on `D_800E9FF0`, `D_800EA0E8`, `D_800F284A` and `gDuel_awPlayerDeck`
  says split, because retail separates each of their `lui` from its use.
- *The byte below `gDuel_bOpponentID` needs a long-lived address holder.*
  Retail reads it as `lui`/`addiu`/`lb -0x1`, three instructions. Any spelling
  whose address pseudo has a single use -- `(&gDuel_bOpponentID)[-1]`, a cast
  through `u8 *`, a local pointer assigned in the same block, even one pinned
  to `$v0` -- lets combine fold the `-1` into the `%lo` and builds one
  instruction short. Assigning the holder at the top of the function keeps the
  pseudo live long enough that the fold does not happen. A `volatile` pointee
  blocks the fold too but then loads with `lbu` and a `sll`, which is one
  instruction long.
- *The shuffle argument pair needs explicit `goto`s.* The `q = p` assignment is
  reached both by the outer `>= 0` branch and by falling out of the inner
  `>= 39` test, and retail shares one copy of it between them. Nested `if`/
  `else` reproduces the shared block only with the branch polarity inverted;
  writing the two early exits as `goto shuffle` gives retail's `bnez`/delay
  slot arrangement and keeps the count. Inverting the polarity without the
  `goto` makes GCC duplicate the block and costs two instructions.
