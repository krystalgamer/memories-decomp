## `func_8006C120` at 0x8006C120

`gcc_2_8_1_g0`, 119 of 119 instructions, opcode distance 0, 8 differing
positions, all in the prologue.

Recursive triangle subdivision. It copies its three corner triplets and the
three edge midpoints into six stack slots through the already-matched
`func_8006C30C` and `func_8006C330`, then either recurses four times on the
sub-triangles `(m0,m5,m4)`, `(m1,m3,m5)`, `(m2,m4,m3)`, `(m3,m4,m5)` or, once
the depth counter reaches 1, writes those same four triangles as twelve
triplets and returns the advanced output pointer.

Three shapes are settled:

- **The last recursive call's value must go to a separate `result` local, not
  back into `out`.** Assigning to `out` and returning it at the end is 120
  instructions; returning the call directly is 118 *and* flips the block
  layout, because an arm ending in a `return` stops being the fall-through and
  the target's `beqz` sense is lost. A distinct `result` assigned in both arms
  gives 119 with the target's layout.
- **`o = out` and `d = depth - 1` belong after the six helper calls**, not
  before them. Moving those two statements above the calls is worth 24
  positions on its own, with no change to the instruction count.
- The six slots are a `Triplet mid[6]` array. Six separate locals measure 43,
  and reversing their declaration order measures 116.

`o` and `d` are pinned to `$s6` and `$s3`. Unpinned the function is 42
differing; the pins are what bring the four saved-register roles into line.

**What is left** is the order of four prologue copies. The target emits them in
parameter order -- `a`, `b`, `c`, `out` -- and the registers are now correct
(`$s0`, `$s2`, `$s1`, `$s6`). This build emits `b`, `c`, `out`, `a`. The cause
is that a pinned local's copy is an ordinary statement, scheduled after the
entry sequence, while an unpinned parameter's copy is emitted at entry. Pinning
`a` to `$16` fixes the register but moves its copy last; leaving it unpinned
keeps the copy at entry but swaps `$s0` and `$s1` with `c` (18 differing, all
that one swap). Both are 8-or-more, and the two cannot currently be had at
once.

*The `a` pin above is the source stored below.* The entry previously stored the
unpinned variant, which measures 18 rather than the 8 in this heading; the two
are one line apart and the prose describes the pinned one, so the pinned one is
now what is here. The unpinned form remains worth knowing as the other end of
the trade, and it is reproduced by deleting the `aa` declaration and assignment
and reading `a` in the six helper calls.

**The choice of which parameter to pin is free, and adding more is worse.**
Pinning `c` to `$17` and leaving `a` alone measures the same 8, with the same
two copies displaced -- so the residual is not about `a`, it is that exactly one
of the two has to be pinned and whichever it is pays with its copy position.
Measured on this base at `gcc_2_8_1_g0`: `a` alone 8, `c` alone 8, `a`+`c` 12,
`a`+`b`+`c` 12, `b`+`c` 10, `b` alone 25, and unpinning `o` as well 18. Nothing
between the two ends of the trade exists in the pin axis.
