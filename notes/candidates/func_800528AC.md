## `func_800528AC` at 0x800528AC

`gcc_2_8_1_g8_split`, 287 of 288 instructions, **opcode multiset distance 1**
(one `addu`), 16 non-relocation word mismatches with two register pins in
place, 68 without them. Written from scratch: no stored candidate, no rows in
`external_attempts.csv`, empty inventory note.

The per-frame tint pass over the ten 0x18-byte requests at `D_800F2B50`, called
once from `func_80059CE4` in `model_cleanup.c`, between the three
`func_800540B4` slot draws and the `func_800556E8` calls. Each live request
holds a start colour, an end colour, an elapsed count and a duration; the
function interpolates the colour
for this frame into the model slot's `field_DC0`, redraws the slot through
`func_800540B4`, then puts back everything it touched and advances the request's
clock.

Per entry:

* bit 0 of the `u16` at `+0` is the live flag, bit 1 picks one of the two
  `D_800F2C40` slots (stride `MODEL_SLOT_SIZE`), bit 2 becomes `D_8009AF9B`
  around the draw, and bits 3 to 7 are the part id `v`.
* the slot must have `field_E1F` set, and
  `Model_HasInsufficientBufferSpace(side, entry->field_13)` must say there is
  room; either check failing skips straight to the clock update.
* `field_DC0`'s four bytes are saved to the stack, then rebuilt as
  `start[n] * (duration - elapsed) / duration + end[n] * elapsed / duration`
  for the three colour bytes with `field_13` in the fourth.
* two passes over `slot->field_E1B` parts call `func_8004DC38`. The first
  overwrites each part record's `+0xC` with `v` and passes the entry's
  `field_0A`; the second puts back the slot's own `field_BF5` and the
  `field_E06` captured before the draw. `field_1E0[j]` is the part pointer
  array, and both passes leave `slot->field_BF5` set to the value they used.
* `D_8009AF9C` gets `&D_800F2B50[i] + 1` and is cleared again straight after
  `func_800540B4`.
* the clock update runs on every live entry, including the two skip paths:
  `field_0C += func_80058E1C()`, and reaching `field_0E` clears the live bit.

## Five things the shape needed

**The base pointer has to go through a local, even though GCC folds it away
again.** Written `D_800F2B50[i].field`, GCC eliminates the loop counter: the
array giv's `add_val` is the symbol itself, which is exactly the case
`maybe_eliminate_biv_1` rewrites, so the loop test becomes
`slt $sN, D_800F2B50+240` and the `slti $v0, $t3, 0xA` on `i` disappears along
with its stack slot. Assigning `table = D_800F2B50;` first and indexing
`table[i]` leaves `i` alive with both it and the byte offset spilled, which is
what retail does - and the final code is identical either way, because the
pointer is constant-folded back into the address before it is emitted. Without
it the build comes out at 292 instructions against 288 rather than 287; it is
the single most load-bearing line here.

**The record pointer must be array-indexed, not walked.** `e = &table[i]`
gives one giv with every field offset folded into it (`0x0`, `0xA`, `0xC`,
`0xE`, `0x10` to `0x16`), which is retail's `$s5`. Walking it as a biv
(`e++` in the increment clause) makes GCC strength-reduce the field addresses
into a *second* pointer at `e + 14` used with offsets -4, -2, 0 and 5, plus its
own increment and its own callee-saved save and restore.

**The first test is a byte load.** `(e->flags & 1) == 0` written against the
`u16` shares one `lhu` with the `(e->flags >> 1) & 1` that follows it. Retail
has `lbu $v0, 0x0($s5)` and then a separate `lhu` at the same address, which is
what `(*(u8 *)e & 1) == 0` produces: the narrowed load is a different mode, so
CSE cannot merge the two. That is one instruction - the difference between
sharing a single `lhu` and issuing both loads - and it was the first residual
found.

**The flags word and the part id are one variable.** Retail's `$s2` is loaded
by `lhu`, shifted in place by `srl $s2, $s2, 3`, and masked in place by
`andi $s2, $s2, 0x1F` in a delay slot 0x94 bytes later. Writing
`v = e->flags; side = (v >> 1) & 1; v = (v >> 3) & 0x1F;` with `v` unsigned
reproduces all three; `u32` matters, since an `s32` gives `sra`.

**The two inner loops need separate counters and explicit `s32` copies of
their `u16` fourth arguments.** Retail hoists both `zero_extend`s out of the
inner loops - `andi $s3, $s3, 0xFFFF` for `field_0A` and
`lhu $s2, 0x24($sp)` for the saved `field_E06`, each once, in the preheader -
and GCC will not do it on its own here, so the conversions are written as
`aa = a;` and `sv = sav06;` before the loops. Reusing one `j` for both loops
also costs two instructions' worth of register naming: retail's first loop has
the counter in `$s1` and the part pointer in `$s0`, and its second loop has
them the other way round, which only happens with two distinct counters.

## What is left is one instruction

The residual is the giv initialiser. Retail materialises the table address into
a scratch register and copies it:

```
lui   $v0, %hi(D_800F2B50)
addiu $v0, $v0, %lo(D_800F2B50)
addu  $s5, $v0, $zero
```

which is `emit_iv_add_mult` reaching `emit_move_insn (giv_reg, add_val)` with
`add_val` still a `REG` - the pointer local surviving as its own pseudo through
`strength_reduce` and past `combine`. Every build here expands the address
straight into the giv register instead:

```
lui   $v0, %hi(D_800F2B50)
addiu $s5, $v0, %lo(D_800F2B50)
```

Nothing tried keeps the copy: eight spellings of the initialiser
(`&D_800F2B50[0]`, `D_800F2B50 + 0`, `(Record *)(s32)D_800F2B50`, an initialiser
on the declaration, `Record *const`, `register`, a `u8 *` base with
`((Record *)base)[i]`, and an `s32` base with `(Record *)(base + i * 0x18)`),
assigning `table` inside the loop so `move_movables` hoists it, a `goto`/label
barrier before the loop to break CSE's constant equivalence, a second
`u8 *base` local for the `D_8009AF9C` expression, and routing the
`D_8009AF9C` expression through `table` so the pointer has two uses. That last
one does reach 288 instructions, but by adding an `addiu $v0, $v0, 1` in the
loop rather than the copy in the prologue - the `+ 1` only folds into
`%lo(D_800F2B50 + 0x1)` when the expression is written against the symbol.

Everything downstream of that one instruction follows from it: retail's scratch
registers sit one higher than this build's (`$t1` and `$t2` where this has `$v0`
and `$t1`), because the surviving pointer pseudo shifts the allocation order.
Fifteen of the sixteen remaining mismatches are that renumbering.

## Pins

Two pins, `register u32 v asm("$18")` and `register s32 aa asm("$19")`, take it
from 68 mismatches to 16 and make the whole body - both inner loops, all six
divides, the `lwl`/`lwr` save and restore pairs, the `%gp_rel` stores and the
loop tail - byte-identical, including every register. They are pure naming:
unpinned, GCC gives `aa` `$s2`, `slot` `$s3`, `e` `$s4` and `v` `$s5`, where
retail has `v` `$s2`, `a` `$s3`, `slot` `$s4` and `e` `$s5`. The instruction
count is 287 either way, so the pins are not what the missing `addu` needs. The
unpinned form is what is stored here.
