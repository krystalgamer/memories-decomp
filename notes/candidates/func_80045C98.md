## `func_80045C98` at 0x80045C98

`gcc_2_8_1_g0`, 169 of 169 instructions, opcode distance 0, **3 differing
positions**, all register names. The two `mult` products used to come back
through `$a2` where retail has `$v0`; naming and pinning them fixes that, and
what is left is the zero-extended first volume sitting in `$v1` for retail's
`$a0`.

The per-frame volume fade in the sound driver, next to `func_80045BE8`.
Three fades share one shape: when the step at +0x1588 (+0x1582, +0x512)
is non-zero it is added to the level at +0x1586 (+0x1580, +0x510), and the
level is clamped to the target byte at +0x158A (+0x1584, +0x49) once it
crosses it in the step's direction, clearing the step. The first two set a
changed flag; when it is set the level at +0x1580 is scaled by the mixer
value at +0x44 and then by the level at +0x1586, each as
`(u16)(a * (b + 1)) >> 8`, and the result goes to `func_80049F10` twice.
The third fade ends with `func_80044DC0` on the new CD volume.

**What the shape needed.**

- *Every access through `g_SDValue` as a struct field, no cached pointer.*
  The halfword `+=` compiles to `lhu; lhu; addu; sh` on its own, and the
  clamp test folds the stored sum back through `sll`/`sra`. The pointer
  reloads at the join labels come from cse's table flush, and the
  branch-into-the-middle jumps (`.L80045D44`) from cse retargeting a
  branch past a reload it can prove redundant.
- *`changed = 1` right after the add, inside the step test,* so it lands
  in the branch delay slot.
- *The two scaled values are ternaries* (`m ? ... : 0`) on a `u16` mixer
  local read once, a `u16` first result (the `andi 0xFFFF` at the join is
  its promotion) and an `s16` second result.
- *The `s16` result local is also used for the CD volume call.* Its load
  from +0x510 gives the pseudo unknown high bits, which is what keeps the
  `sll`/`sra` sign extension before `func_80049F10`; with the local set
  only from the shifted products combine proves the value fits and folds
  the extension to a move.

**Where the residual is.** The product pseudo of each `mult` has a single
register use, so regclass prefers `LO_REG` for it, local allocation skips
it (`CLASS_LIKELY_SPILLED_P`), global allocation puts it in `LO`, and
reload materialises it through a fresh spill register, the first unused
caller-saved one, `$a2`. Retail's `mflo $v0` needs the product to be an
ordinary `GR_REGS` pseudo, which happens as soon as it has a second
register use (verified with a dummy compare: `mflo $a0`), but no spelling
found gives it a second use without extra instructions. Crossed: `s32`
product locals, one shared and one per block; `(u16)`, `& 0xFFFF` and
`(u32)(u16)` truncations; `u16` intermediates for the truncated product;
if/else instead of the ternaries. The `$a0`/`$v1` choice for the first
result follows the same allocation.

### Naming the multiply product removes four of the seven

Retail computes each product straight into `$v0`
(`mult v1,v0` / `mflo v0` / `andi v0,v0,0xffff`); the candidate returned it
through `$a2` and then moved it (`mflo a2` / `andi v0,a2,0xffff`). The
expression is a conditional, `x = m ? (u16)(m * (SD->field_1580 + 1)) >> 8 : 0`,
so the product has no name to constrain.

Rewriting the two conditionals as `if`/`else` is neutral on its own, but it
creates a scope in which the product *can* be named. Declaring it there and
pinning it - `register u16 p1 asm("$2") = (u16)(m * (SD->field_1580 + 1));` -
takes the candidate from **7 to 3**. `$3` costs 11 and `$6` 62, so it is the
specific register that matters, not the naming.

This is the same lever that took `func_80023144` from 7 to 5: an *allocation*
disagreement is reachable by giving the value a name and a register, even when
the value is an anonymous temporary in the original spelling. Splitting a
conditional expression into a statement `if` is the cheapest way to create a
place to put that declaration; it did not change a single instruction here.

### What remains is dead-register reuse, and it resists

The three left are all the same value: `(u16)x`, which the candidate keeps in
`$v1` and retail in `$a0`. `$v1` holds `m` immediately before, `m` is dead by
then, and GCC reuses it; retail takes a fresh register. Measured and rejected:
pinning `x` to `$4` or `$5` costs 67, because the hard register is then treated
as live across `func_80049F10(y, y)` and the allocator spills around the call.
Introducing a separate truncation variable pinned to `$4`, initialised either in
its declaration or by assignment, is neutral - GCC coalesces it with `x` and
keeps `$v1`. Moving `m` to six different registers is neutral, because `m` is
loaded straight into `$v1` by `lhu` and the pin does not take. Scoping `m` and
`x` to the `if (changed)` block, reversing their declaration order, and adding
a spare local before them are all neutral.
